# illoca

> illoca builds Tracing Paper, an AI-native design engine for professional architects.

Tracing Paper helps architects move from early ideas to editable 2D and 3D designs. It supports sketches, redlines, prompts, floor plans, massing studies, iterative refinements, and exports for professional design workflows.
